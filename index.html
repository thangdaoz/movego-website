How it works

Prop up. Step back. Play.

A free guided practice teaches every move before your first run. It is hands-free, so you can start standing up.

  1. 1

    Prop up your phone

    Lean it on a shelf or put it on a stand at about waist height, with the front camera facing you.

  2. 2

    Step back 2–3 m

    Fit your whole body in the frame. A bright room helps.

  3. 3

    Move to play

    Jog in place to run. Jump, duck, step and punch to dodge what comes at you.

  • Jog in placeRun on the spot and dodge.
  • Dodge onlyStand and dodge, no jogging.
Six moves

Try the six moves.

Pick a move. See what your body does, then what the game asks for.

Jump

Jump over hurdles and barriers.

  • Jump: jump over hurdles and barriers.
  • Duck: duck under bars and gates.
  • Step left: step left to dodge blocks and grab coins.
  • Step right: step right to clear crates and walls.
  • Punch: throw a punch to smash what is in your way.
  • Pose: match the outline on the wall to pass through.

Or play on your TV.

The phone alone is enough. If you want a bigger screen, mirror your iPhone to a TV with Screen Mirroring (AirPlay).

Your camera stays on your phone.

MOVEGO uses the front camera to see your moves. It does that on your phone.

  • Processed on your phone

    Pose detection runs on your iPhone, not on a server.

  • Never uploaded or saved

    Camera images and video are not uploaded, saved or shared.

  • No account needed

    Start as a guest. Sign in with Apple only if you want to keep progress across devices.

Free & Plus

Free to start. Plus for unlimited runs.

MOVEGO Free

Everything you need to try it

  • 1 free energy every day, refilled at 00:00 UTC
  • Each run uses 1 energy
  • Earn more energy with coins and invites
  • Guided practice for every move

MOVEGO Plus

For people who play every day

  • No energy limit: play as many runs as you like
  • Weekly, every 3 months or yearly
  • Does not change scores or ranks
  • Auto-renews until you cancel

Prices and any free trial are shown in the app before you buy. Cancel any time in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MOVEGO.

Before you play

What you need.

  • An iPhone

    With a front camera, on iOS 16.4 or later. MOVEGO is iPhone only.

  • About 2 m of space

    Clear the floor around you. Stand 2–3 m back from the phone.

  • A spot for the phone

    A shelf, stand or tripod at about waist height.

  • Good light

    Use a bright room and avoid a strong light behind you.

Play safely. Use a non-slip floor. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. Ask a doctor first if you have a health condition or are pregnant. Children should play with an adult nearby. MOVEGO is a game: calorie and distance numbers are estimates, not medical advice. Rated 12+.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Cannot find your answer? Visit Support.

Do I need a controller, a wearable or a TV?

No. You need an iPhone with a front camera. Your body is the controller. A TV is optional.

Is my camera recorded?

No. Pose detection runs on your phone. Camera images and video are never uploaded, saved or shared.

How much space do I need?

Clear about 2 meters around you. Prop the phone up at waist height and stand 2–3 meters back, so your whole body is in view.

Is MOVEGO free?

It is free to start. Free players get 1 energy every day, and each run uses 1 energy. You can earn more with coins and invites. MOVEGO Plus removes the energy limit.

Do I need an account?

No. Play as a guest. Sign in with Apple if you want to keep your progress on any device.

How do I play on a TV?

Mirror your iPhone to a TV with Screen Mirroring (AirPlay). Your phone camera still tracks you, so stand 2–3 meters back from the phone.

Does MOVEGO count calories?

It shows estimates of active calories and distance. They are not medical advice.

Does MOVEGO work with Apple Health?

Yes, if you turn it on. Apple Health is optional and write-only: MOVEGO can save your workouts there and does not read your health data.

Is there an Android version?

No. MOVEGO is for iPhone only.

How do I cancel Plus or delete my data?

To cancel, open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MOVEGO.

To delete your account, open Settings in the app, tap your profile and choose Delete account. More help is on the Support page.

Who can play?

MOVEGO is rated 12+. Children should play with an adult nearby.

Ready to move?

MOVEGO is free to start. Prop up your phone and play your first run.
