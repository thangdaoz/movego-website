Play safely

  • MOVEGO involves jumping, ducking, stepping and punching. Clear about 2 meters of space around you and play on a non-slip floor.
  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath. Talk to a doctor before you start if you have a health condition, are pregnant or have not exercised in a long time.
  • Children should play with an adult nearby.
  • MOVEGO is a game. Calorie and distance numbers are estimates and are not medical advice.

You play at your own risk. To the extent the law allows, we are not responsible for injuries or damage that happen while you play.

Accounts

You can play as a guest or sign in with Apple. Keep your device secure. Do not cheat, use automated input or try to interfere with ranks or other players. We may remove scores or accounts that break these rules.

Display names

Your display name is shown to your friends and to all players on the global rank. Do not use names that are offensive, impersonate someone or break the law. We may change or remove such names. You can report or block any player from the rank.

Coins and energy

Coins and energy are in-game items with no cash value. They cannot be sold, transferred or exchanged for money.

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
  • If a plan includes a free trial, you are charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. A trial is available once per Apple ID.
