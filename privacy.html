Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

MOVEGO is an exercise game made by Dao Duc Thang ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: nakroth2410@gmail.com.

Your camera stays on your phone

MOVEGO uses the front camera to see your body movements. Pose detection runs on your device. Camera images and video are never uploaded, saved or shared, by us or by any service we use.

Age, height and weight that you enter stay on your device. They are used only to estimate calories.

If you turn on Apple Health in Settings, MOVEGO saves each finished run to Apple Health as a workout (type, start and end time). MOVEGO does not read anything from Apple Health, and this data is not sent to us or used for advertising.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, health records or advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase: accounts and the game database (runs, coins, energy, friends).
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and App Store payments.
  • RevenueCat: subscription status. It receives your user ID and App Store purchase records, not your payment card.
  • Sentry: crash and performance reports, without your name or email.
  • PostHog: app interaction events, linked to your user ID only (never your email). Screen recording is off.

These services may store data in the United States or other countries. They process it only to provide their service to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use MOVEGO. When you delete your account or your guest data, we delete it from our database. Crash reports and analytics events expire on the providers' standard schedules (90 days to 1 year).

Your choices

  • Delete your account: Settings → tap your profile → Delete account. This deletes your profile, runs, coins, energy and friends, and revokes Sign in with Apple.
  • Guests: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete local data removes your data from the device and from our server.
  • Camera: you can turn camera access off at any time in iOS Settings. The game needs it to play.
  • Apple Health: turn it off in Settings → Apple Health, or in the Health app. Workouts already saved stay in Apple Health until you delete them there.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel them in your App Store account settings. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion by email.

Children

MOVEGO is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and change the date at the top.
