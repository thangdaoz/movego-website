The game does not see me

  • Prop the phone up at about waist height and step back about 2 meters, so your whole body is in view.
  • Use a bright room and avoid a strong light behind you.
  • Check that camera access is on: iOS Settings → MOVEGO → Camera.

Subscriptions

  • Cancel or change your plan: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → MOVEGO.
  • Bought Plus on another device? In the app, open Plus and tap Restore purchases.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete your data

Account: Settings → tap your profile → Delete account. Guest: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete local data. See the Privacy Policy.
